V8 Redline Studio (the “Operator”) values the privacy of users of the mobile game ForgeLine (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it is used, how long it is retained, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Information processed automatically or as necessary to provide the Service

  • Account information: internal member identifier, guest account identifier, nickname, and sign-in method.
  • Linked sign-in information: Google Play Games or Apple authentication tokens and account identifiers supplied by the provider. The Operator does not collect passwords for social accounts.
  • Game information: progression, stage records, currencies, equipment, decks, attendance, events, rankings, reward claims, and anti-abuse verification records.
  • Purchase information: product ID, order or transaction identifier, purchase receipt or verification token, and purchase, refund, or restoration status. Payment card details are processed by the applicable app marketplace and are not collected directly by the Operator.
  • Device and connection information: IP address, access time, operating system and app version, device model, language and country settings, device or app identifiers, advertising identifiers, network information, error logs, and diagnostics.
  • Advertising information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views, rewarded-ad completion records, advertising consent choices, and anti-fraud information.
  • Security information: app and device integrity verdicts, request signatures and nonces, suspicious access records, and enforcement history.

Information processed when you contact us

  • Email address, inquiry details, account-identifying information, and screenshots or files that you choose to attach.

The Service does not require access to government identification numbers, contacts, street addresses, precise location, photos, microphones, or contact lists for its core features. If future features change the information processed, this Policy and the relevant app marketplace disclosures will be updated before those changes take effect.

2. Purposes of Processing

  • Creating, authenticating, linking, recovering, and deleting accounts.
  • Saving game progress and operating content, currencies, items, rankings, and events.
  • Verifying, granting, restoring, and refunding in-app purchases and preventing duplicate fulfillment.
  • Providing rewarded advertisements, managing privacy choices, and verifying ad rewards.
  • Diagnosing errors, improving performance and quality, and responding to support requests.
  • Detecting cheating, automation, payment abuse, advertising abuse, and other misuse.
  • Meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes.

5. International Data Transfers

When global services provided by Google, Apple, Unity, and other providers are used, personal information may be transmitted to or stored on servers outside the Republic of Korea. Transfers may occur when the Service is used, an ad is requested, a linked sign-in is performed, or a purchase is processed. Data is encrypted in transit. The specific recipient country, recipient, transferred items, and retention period depend on each provider’s infrastructure and contractual arrangements and will be specified based on the final production configuration before launch.

6. Advertising Identifiers and Personalized Advertising

The Service may offer rewarded advertisements that a user chooses to view. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention.

  • In applicable regions, users can manage privacy choices through the Google User Messaging Platform consent form.
  • On iOS, users can decide whether to permit tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Users can reset or delete advertising identifiers and change tracking permissions through Android or iOS system settings.
  • Declining consent does not prevent access to the core game, but may affect ad personalization or availability.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent concerning your personal information.

  • Use the account deletion function in the in-game account management screen.
  • Follow the Account and Data Deletion instructions to submit an email request.
  • Use the advertising privacy choices form or your operating system settings to change advertising and tracking choices.

We may request the minimum information necessary to verify account ownership. Information subject to a legal retention obligation may not be deleted until the applicable retention period expires.

8. Children

The Service is not primarily directed to children under 14, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 without the consent of a legal guardian. If the Operator learns that such information has been collected, appropriate action will be taken under applicable law. The final target age and applicability of app marketplace family policies will be confirmed before release.

9. Security Measures

  • Encrypted communications such as TLS.
  • Server-side authorization and request-tampering protections.
  • Server verification and duplicate-fulfillment prevention for purchases and ad rewards.
  • Least-privilege access and operational access logging.
  • Security incident response and vulnerability review.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated when laws, Service features, or third-party SDKs change. Material changes will be announced in the Service or on this page before they take effect.
