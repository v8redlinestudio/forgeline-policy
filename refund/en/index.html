1. Scope

This Policy applies to paid digital content purchased in ForgeLine through Google Play or Apple App Store. Cancellation and refund requests are handled under applicable law, the relevant marketplace policy, and this Policy. Mandatory legal and marketplace rights prevail.

2. Withdrawal

You may request withdrawal within seven days after receiving the contract information, or within seven days after supply begins if supply begins later. If content differs from its description or the contract, a request may be made within three months after supply or within 30 days after discovering the discrepancy.

Withdrawal may be limited, only where legally permitted and clearly disclosed before purchase, for digital content supplied immediately or already used, partially consumed bundles, or virtual currency that you explicitly consumed. Purchases by minors without required guardian consent may be subject to statutory cancellation rights.

3. Common Refund Situations

  • Payment completed but the purchased product was not delivered.
  • A system error caused duplicate payment or fulfillment.
  • The delivered content materially differs from the advertised product.
  • A refund right is recognized under applicable law or marketplace policy.

Delivery errors may first be resolved through server-record verification, redelivery, or purchase restoration. Content that was correctly delivered and consumed may be ineligible for refund unless applicable law or marketplace policy provides otherwise.

4. How to Request Help

  1. Use the refund or report-a-problem process in your Google Play or Apple purchase history.
  2. For missing fulfillment, restoration, or transaction verification, email rivergameforge@gmail.com.
  3. Include your nickname, internal user ID, platform, product, order or transaction ID, purchase time, and issue. Do not send card numbers or account passwords.

5. Purchase Restoration

Eligible non-consumable products and subscriptions can be restored through the app or marketplace restoration feature. Consumable currencies are generally not marketplace-restorable, so server fulfillment records are checked. Receipts and transaction identifiers may be verified to prevent duplicate grants.

6. After a Refund

When a refund is approved, virtual currency, items, or benefits granted by that purchase may be reversed. If already consumed, an account-balance adjustment or restriction may be applied only within applicable law and prior disclosure, with an opportunity to appeal through support. Transaction review and user explanation are prioritized over automatic enforcement.

7. Timing

Refund timing and payment method for marketplace-managed requests follow the applicable marketplace policy. The Operator reviews fulfillment and restoration requests after account and transaction verification; additional evidence or marketplace confirmation may extend processing time.
